## About N9raw

**N9raw** is building a trusted student platform for Morocco, designed around the student’s **stage, intent and next useful action**.

**Étudier · S’orienter · Opportunités · Étudier à l’étranger · S’informer**

## Trust by design

Important public information is designed to be sourced, dated, reviewed and correctable. French and Arabic/RTL are first-class experiences, with accessibility and privacy treated as product requirements.

**Nour FPN** is its first faculty-specific implementation for students at FPN Nador.
